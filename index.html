About the Group

The Simulation and Control of Sustainable Energy Systems (SIMCON) Group at NSUT, Delhi, conducts advanced computational research at the intersection of modern power systems, smart grid frameworks, and automated control theory. Operating entirely in the high-fidelity simulation domain, the Group is exploring the mathematical complexities of decentralized architectures. Key investigative fields include grid-interactive building infrastructures, demand-side management configurations, and multi-area network resilience. SIMCON Group specializes in engineering intelligent frequency regulation algorithms and pioneering peer-to-peer (P2P) localized energy trading mechanisms. Backed by mathematical optimization and soft computing, our workflows evaluate the real-world operational thresholds of next-generation grids facing massive modernization challenges, such as the accelerating growth of data infrastructures and distributed energy integration.

Overall Research Goal: To deliver highly secure, scalable, and resilient mathematical control frameworks that protect modern power systems against cyber-adversaries, mitigate stochastic vulnerabilities, and seamlessly optimize renewable-dominated microgrids.

Research Directions

Frequency regulation Intelligent controllers for multi-area and multi-source systems.
Demand-side management Thermostatically controlled loads as virtual energy storage.
Grid-interactive buildings Occupant-centric, comfort-aware control of building loads.
P2P energy trading Dynamic pricing mechanisms for localized microgrid markets.
Cyber-physical resilience Control under DoS attacks, time delays and stochastic faults.
Data centre loads Impact of hyperscale AI infrastructure on grid architecture.
