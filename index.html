About the Group

The Simulation and Control of Sustainable Energy Systems (SIMCON) Group at NSUT, Delhi, conducts advanced computational research at the intersection of modern power systems, smart grid frameworks, and automated control theory. Operating entirely in the high-fidelity simulation domain, the Group is exploring the mathematical complexities of decentralized architectures. Key investigative fields include grid-interactive building infrastructures, demand-side management configurations, and multi-area network resilience. SIMCON Group specializes in engineering intelligent frequency regulation algorithms and pioneering peer-to-peer (P2P) localized energy trading mechanisms. Backed by mathematical optimization and soft computing, our workflows evaluate the real-world operational thresholds of next-generation grids facing massive modernization challenges, such as the accelerating growth of data infrastructures and distributed energy integration.

Overall Research Goal: To deliver highly secure, scalable, and resilient mathematical control frameworks that protect modern power systems against cyber-adversaries, mitigate stochastic vulnerabilities, and seamlessly optimize renewable-dominated microgrids.

Research Directions

Power system dynamics & control

Load-frequency control, multi-area and multi-source systems, delay-dependent and nonlinear control, stability analysis.

Demand-side flexibility & buildings

Thermostatically controlled loads and inverter air-conditioners as virtual energy storage, with comfort-aware occupant-centric control.

Renewable energy & microgrids

Solar photovoltaics, wind, battery storage, small hydro and distributed generation in renewable-integrated microgrids.

Cyber-physical resilience

Secure and safety-critical control under denial-of-service attacks, communication delays and stochastic faults.

EVs, distribution & emerging loads

EV charging infrastructure and its impact on distribution systems, active distribution planning, and hyperscale AI data-centre loads.

AI, optimization & intelligent systems

Deep learning for power-quality classification, evolutionary and hybrid metaheuristics, soft computing and intelligent controllers.

P2P energy systems & markets

Peer-to-peer trading, supply-demand ratio pricing and demand-side management in localized microgrid markets.

Autonomous systems & robotics

Unmanned ground vehicles, 2D and 3D LiDAR mapping, path planning and trajectory optimization.
