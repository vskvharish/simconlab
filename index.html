SIMCON Lab

Simulation and Control of Sustainable Energy Systems Laboratory
Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT), Delhi, India

The SIMCON Lab at NSUT, Delhi, develops simulation and advanced control frameworks for resilient smart grids. Our computational research spans cyber-physical security, brain-inspired frequency regulation, P2P energy trading, building energy systems, and the grid impacts of hyperscale AI data centers.

Active Research Projects

  • Impact of hyperscale AI data centres on the grid — Ms. Prerna Yadav (PhD Researcher)
  • Modelling and control of nonlinear dynamical stochastic systems under cyber attack — B.Tech Cohort
  • Occupants-centric control of building energy systems — B.Tech Cohort
  • Multi energy systems with pico/micro scaled pumped hydro storage — B.Tech Cohort

Key Publication Domains & Preprints

  • Brain-Inspired Frequency Regulation & Dynamic P2P Pricing (Applied Energy / ASME)
